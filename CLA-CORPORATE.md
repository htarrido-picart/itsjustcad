# Contributor License Agreement (Corporate / Entity)

This Corporate CLA covers contributions made to ItsJustCAD by employees or
agents of a legal entity. It complements the individual CLA (`CLA.md`): each
individual still signs the individual CLA, and this agreement binds the entity
that owns their work.

**This is a plain-language summary followed by the operative grant. The entity
should have this reviewed by its own counsel. The project owner should have this
reviewed by an IP attorney before relying on it.**

## In plain terms

- Your company grants Hector Tarrido-Picart ("the Owner") the right to use your
  employees' contributions in the open-source (AGPLv3-only) editions **and** in
  proprietary commercial / mobile editions — i.e. the right to dual-license.
- You confirm your company has the right to grant this for the contributions
  made by the people you list.
- Your company keeps ownership of its contributions.

2. **Patent license.** You grant the Owner and recipients of the software a

   the contribution infringes a patent.

3. **Authority.** You represent that You have the legal right to grant the
   licenses above for contributions made by Your employees and agents, and that
   doing so does not violate any agreement or any third party's rights.

4. **Authorized contributors.** You will maintain, by the agreed mechanism
   (e.g. an updated schedule or the signature record), the list of individuals
   authorized to contribute on Your behalf, and will notify the Owner of
   changes. Each such individual also agrees to the individual `CLA.md`.

5. **Originality.** To Your knowledge, each contribution is original to Your
   authorized contributors or You otherwise have the right to submit it under
   these terms, and it does not knowingly infringe any third party's rights.

6. **Moral rights.** To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, You and

7. **No obligation; ownership retained.** The Owner is not obligated to use any
   contribution. This agreement does not transfer ownership; it grants the
   licenses above, supported by mutual consideration.

## How to sign

An authorized signatory of the entity records the entity's agreement (entity
name, signatory, date, and this file's git hash) via the mechanism described in
`CONTRIBUTING.md`. That record is the durable proof of what the entity agreed
to. For questions or to arrange signature, contact the Owner:
