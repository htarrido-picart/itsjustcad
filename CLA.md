## How to sign

Signing is handled automatically in your first pull request: a bot will ask you
to comment your agreement, and your signature (GitHub identity, timestamp, and
the version of this CLA) is recorded in `.github/cla-signatures.json`. The CLA
check is a **required status check** on the default branch — a pull request
cannot be merged until the signature is recorded, which closes the race where
unsigned code could reach a protected branch. That record, plus this file's git
hash, is the durable proof of what you agreed to.

Contributors are credited in `AUTHORS`.
